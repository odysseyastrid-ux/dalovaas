# Magicstick Clean — Marketing playbook

Living marketing brief for Magicstick Clean (Rockland / Clarence-Rockland, ON +
Ottawa + Gatineau). Read alongside `CLAUDE.md` for the full project context.
Goal: take a brand-new, unknown cleaning business from 0 → 100 sales and make it
**the best-known cleaner in Rockland**.

Honesty rules (never break): never invent reviews, ratings, prices, insurance, or
stats. Public content ships bilingual FR/EN. Lead with French in this market.

---

## 1. Competitive synthesis (Rockland residential cleaning)

**Market:** ~25k people, strongly **Francophone** (Prescott-Russell), tight-knit,
semi-urban. Word-of-mouth, Google, and local Facebook groups drive decisions more
than mass advertising. Being *genuinely local + bilingual* is a weapon.

### Competitors & how they sit in the customer's mind

- **Molly Maid** (Ottawa-Orléans franchise, serves Rockland from a distance) —
  "the big reassuring brand." Strengths: national brand, insured image, long hours,
  systematized. **Weaknesses to exploit:** not local to Rockland (Ottawa number/
  office), English-only website in a French town, **rotating teams (never the same
  cleaner)**, no prices shown (quote-gated), corporate/cold.
- **Splendid Cleaning / Double Green Maids / Marissa Cleans** (local Rockland) —
  "the good little local you get referred to." Strengths: truly local, bilingual,
  good ratings (Splendid 5.0★). **Weaknesses:** barely known (Splendid ~10 reviews),
  no online booking, no transparent pricing, weak web presence, limited capacity.
- **Informal** (2-person Facebook teams from ~$140, Housekeeper.com individuals
  ~$18/h, Bark $120–250) — "cheap but a risk." Strengths: cheap, flexible.
  **Weaknesses:** no brand, no guarantee, no insurance, unreliable, no site.
- **Marketplaces** (Bark, Yelp, Google) — a *channel* to be present & well-rated on.

### The gap to own (positioning)
Nobody owns the intersection. Magicstick plants its flag here:

> **The reliability of a brand + the intimacy of a Rockland neighbour + the ease of
> 2026 (clear $33/h price, book online in 2 min, same-day reply), in French and
> English.**

### How to beat them
1. **Own the word "Rockland."** *"De Rockland, pour Rockland" / "Your Rockland
   neighbour, not an Ottawa franchise."* (kills Molly Maid's #1 weakness)
2. **Own "the same cleaner every time."** Molly Maid structurally can't promise it.
3. **Own transparency.** They hide prices; we show **$33/h, from $99, instant
   estimate** on the site.
4. **Own French.** Their page is English-only in a Francophone town. Lead in French.
5. **Manufacture social proof fast.** Our only real weakness vs. locals: **0
   reviews.** Turn the first 5–10 clients into **5★ Google reviews** ASAP.

### Advertising that fits this market
- **Foundation first:** optimized **Google Business Profile** + review collection.
- **Local social:** Facebook/Instagram **before/after** + **Rockland community groups.**
- **Hyper-local physical:** **vehicle decal** (rolling billboard in a small town),
  flyers / door hangers in targeted neighbourhoods.
- **Paid, targeted:** Facebook Ads, Rockland radius ($10–15/day) → quote form; later
  **Google Search** for hot intent.
- **Partnerships:** realtors (move-in/out), Airbnb hosts, contractors (post-construction).
- **Trust levers:** referral program + an honest satisfaction guarantee.

**0 → 100 sales arc:** Month 1 = foundations + first 10 clients + reviews. Month 2 =
steady social + first paid campaign + referral program. Month 3 = own "Rockland
cleaning" on Google + self-sustaining word-of-mouth.

## 3. Content batch #1 — 10 bilingual before/after posts

Cadence: 2–3 per week. Each post = hook + FR caption + EN caption + visual + CTA.
Use only real photos already in `assets/images/`. CTA link: magicstickclean.ca
(quote) unless noted. Default hashtags:
`#Rockland #ClarenceRockland #Ottawa #Gatineau #ménageRockland #housecleaning #cleaningservice #Prescott Russell`

### Post 1 — Before/After kitchen (flagship)
- **Hook FR:** « Même cuisine. 3 heures d'écart. » · **EN:** "Same kitchen. 3 hours apart."
- **FR:** Avant → après, sans effort de ton côté. On s'occupe de tout, pendant que tu récupères ta soirée. Ménage à partir de 33 $/h à Rockland. Soumission gratuite, réponse le jour même. 👉 magicstickclean.ca
- **EN:** Before → after, with zero effort on your end. We handle it all while you get your evening back. Cleaning from $33/h in Rockland. Free quote, same-day reply. 👉 magicstickclean.ca
- **Visuel:** avant/après cuisine (slider ou 2 photos côte à côte).

### Post 2 — Same cleaner every time (anti-franchise wedge)
- **Hook FR:** « Chez nous, c'est toujours la même personne qui nettoie chez toi. » · **EN:** "With us, it's always the same person cleaning your home."
- **FR:** Pas une équipe différente chaque semaine. La même personne de confiance, qui finit par connaître ta maison par cœur. C'est ça, la différence d'un vrai local. 🏠
- **EN:** Not a different crew every week. The same trusted person who ends up knowing your home by heart. That's the local difference. 🏠
- **Visuel:** photo de toi au travail (baseboard/detail) ou la photo « famille + chien ».

### Post 3 — Transparent price (anti-quote-gating)
- **Hook FR:** « 33 $/h. Voilà. Pas de surprise. » · **EN:** "$33/h. That's it. No surprises."
- **FR:** Chez certains, il faut appeler juste pour connaître le prix. Nous, c'est clair : 33 $/h, minimum 3 h, dépôt remboursable. Tu peux même calculer ton estimation en direct sur le site. 👉 magicstickclean.ca
- **EN:** Some make you call just to learn the price. We keep it clear: $33/h, 3-hour minimum, refundable deposit. You can even get a live estimate right on the site. 👉 magicstickclean.ca
- **Visuel:** capture de l'estimateur du site.

### Post 4 — Local Rockland pride
- **Hook FR:** « De Rockland, pour Rockland. 🍁 » · **EN:** "From Rockland, for Rockland. 🍁"
- **FR:** On n'est pas une franchise qui vient d'Ottawa. On habite ici, on nettoie ici, on répond en français. Soutiens un service local qui te répond vraiment.
- **EN:** We're not a franchise driving in from Ottawa. We live here, we clean here, we answer in French or English. Support a local service that actually answers.
- **Visuel:** photo locale / véhicule / logo sur fond quartier.

### Post 5 — Bathroom before/after
- **Hook FR:** « Ta salle de bain mérite ce niveau. » · **EN:** "Your bathroom deserves this level."
- **FR:** Tartre, joints, miroirs : on va dans les détails que les autres sautent. Avant → après. 👉 Réserve ton créneau : magicstickclean.ca
- **EN:** Limescale, grout, mirrors: we get into the details others skip. Before → after. 👉 Book your slot: magicstickclean.ca
- **Visuel:** avant/après salle de bain.

### Post 6 — Fridge / detail before/after (satisfying)
- **Hook FR:** « Le genre d'avant/après qui fait du bien à regarder. » · **EN:** "The kind of before/after that's oddly satisfying."
- **FR:** On adore les détails qu'on oublie : l'intérieur du frigo, les plinthes, les recoins. Et toi, qu'est-ce que tu détestes nettoyer ? Dis-le en commentaire 👇
- **EN:** We love the forgotten details: inside the fridge, baseboards, corners. What's the one chore you hate most? Tell us below 👇
- **Visuel:** avant/après frigo. (Engagement = commentaires → portée.)

### Post 7 — Recurring plan (build recurring revenue)
- **Hook FR:** « Une maison propre, chaque semaine, sans y penser. » · **EN:** "A clean home, every week, without thinking about it."
- **FR:** Nos clients réguliers passent à **30 $/h (9 % de rabais)**, tant que le forfait est maintenu. Hebdo, aux deux semaines ou mensuel — à toi de choisir. 👉 magicstickclean.ca
- **EN:** Regulars get **$30/h (9% off)**, for as long as the plan runs. Weekly, biweekly or monthly — your call. 👉 magicstickclean.ca
- **Visuel:** calendrier stylisé + salon propre.

### Post 8 — Move-in / move-out (partnership angle, realtors/renters)
- **Hook FR:** « Tu déménages ? Récupère ton dépôt, pas le stress. » · **EN:** "Moving out? Get your deposit back, not the stress."
- **FR:** Nettoyage entrée/sortie à Rockland. On laisse le logement impeccable pour l'inspection ou les nouveaux occupants. Agents immobiliers : parlons partenariat. 📩
- **EN:** Move-in / move-out cleaning in Rockland. We leave the place spotless for inspection or new occupants. Realtors: let's talk partnership. 📩
- **Visuel:** photo move-in/out.

### Post 9 — Gift card (seasonal / referral)
- **Hook FR:** « Le cadeau que tout le monde veut vraiment : du temps. » · **EN:** "The gift everyone actually wants: time."
- **FR:** Offre un ménage à quelqu'un que tu aimes. Carte-cadeau Magicstick Clean, code unique utilisable à la réservation. 👉 magicstickclean.ca/gift-cards.html
- **EN:** Gift a clean home to someone you love. Magicstick Clean gift card, unique code redeemable at booking. 👉 magicstickclean.ca/gift-cards.html
- **Visuel:** visuel carte-cadeau.

### Post 10 — Ask for the review (turn first clients into proof)
- **Hook FR:** « Merci à nos premiers clients de Rockland. 🙏 » · **EN:** "Thank you to our first Rockland clients. 🙏"
- **FR:** On démarre, et chaque client compte énormément. Si on a bien nettoyé chez toi, ton avis Google aide ton voisin à nous faire confiance. Ça prend 30 secondes — et ça veut tout dire pour un nouveau local. ⭐
- **EN:** We're just starting, and every client means the world. If we did right by you, your Google review helps your neighbour trust us. It takes 30 seconds — and it means everything to a new local business. ⭐
- **Visuel:** photo propre + ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (gabarit, PAS de faux avis — simple invitation).
- **Note:** poste-le seulement une fois que tu AS des premiers clients satisfaits.
