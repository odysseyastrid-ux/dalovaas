# Magicstick Clean — Marketing playbook

Living marketing brief for Magicstick Clean (Rockland / Clarence-Rockland, ON +
Ottawa + Gatineau). Read alongside `CLAUDE.md` for the full project context.
Goal: take a brand-new, unknown cleaning business from 0 → 100 sales and make it
**the best-known cleaner in Rockland**.

Honesty rules (never break): never invent reviews, ratings, prices, insurance, or
stats. Public content ships bilingual FR/EN. Lead with French in this market.

---

## 1. Competitive synthesis (Rockland residential cleaning)

**Market:** ~25k people, strongly **Francophone** (Prescott-Russell), tight-knit,
semi-urban. Word-of-mouth, Google, and local Facebook groups drive decisions more
than mass advertising. Being *genuinely local + bilingual* is a weapon.

### Competitors & how they sit in the customer's mind

- **Molly Maid** (Ottawa-Orléans franchise, serves Rockland from a distance) —
  "the big reassuring brand." Strengths: national brand, insured image, long hours,
  systematized. **Weaknesses to exploit:** not local to Rockland (Ottawa number/
  office), English-only website in a French town, **rotating teams (never the same
  cleaner)**, no prices shown (quote-gated), corporate/cold.
- **Splendid Cleaning / Double Green Maids / Marissa Cleans** (local Rockland) —
  "the good little local you get referred to." Strengths: truly local, bilingual,
  good ratings (Splendid 5.0★). **Weaknesses:** barely known (Splendid ~10 reviews),
  no online booking, no transparent pricing, weak web presence, limited capacity.
- **Informal** (2-person Facebook teams from ~$140, Housekeeper.com individuals
  ~$18/h, Bark $120–250) — "cheap but a risk." Strengths: cheap, flexible.
  **Weaknesses:** no brand, no guarantee, no insurance, unreliable, no site.
- **Marketplaces** (Bark, Yelp, Google) — a *channel* to be present & well-rated on.

### The gap to own (positioning)
Nobody owns the intersection. Magicstick plants its flag here:

> **The reliability of a brand + the intimacy of a Rockland neighbour + the ease of
> 2026 (clear $30/h price, book online in 2 min, same-day reply), in French and
> English.**

### How to beat them
1. **Own the word "Rockland."** *"De Rockland, pour Rockland" / "Your Rockland
   neighbour, not an Ottawa franchise."* (kills Molly Maid's #1 weakness)
2. **Own "the same cleaner every time."** Molly Maid structurally can't promise it.
3. **Own transparency.** They hide prices; we show **$30/h, from $90, instant
   estimate** on the site.
4. **Own French.** Their page is English-only in a Francophone town. Lead in French.
5. **Manufacture social proof fast.** Our only real weakness vs. locals: **0
   reviews.** Turn the first 5–10 clients into **5★ Google reviews** ASAP.

### Advertising that fits this market
- **Foundation first:** optimized **Google Business Profile** + review collection.
- **Local social:** Facebook/Instagram **before/after** + **Rockland community groups.**
- **Hyper-local physical:** **vehicle decal** (rolling billboard in a small town),
  flyers / door hangers in targeted neighbourhoods.
- **Paid, targeted:** Facebook Ads, Rockland radius ($10–15/day) → quote form; later
  **Google Search** for hot intent.
- **Partnerships:** realtors (move-in/out), Airbnb hosts, contractors (post-construction).
- **Trust levers:** referral program + an honest satisfaction guarantee.

**0 → 100 sales arc:** Month 1 = foundations + first 10 clients + reviews. Month 2 =
steady social + first paid campaign + referral program. Month 3 = own "Rockland
cleaning" on Google + self-sustaining word-of-mouth.

## 5. Batch #3 — Google Business Profile optimization (your #1 lever)

GBP is what makes you show up on Google Maps for "cleaning near me." For a local
service it beats almost everything else. Do this first.

**Setup checklist**
- [ ] Create/claim the profile at google.com/business and **verify** it.
- [ ] Business name: **Magicstick Clean** (exact, no keyword stuffing).
- [ ] **Service-area business** (hide address): list Clarence-Rockland, Rockland,
      Bourget, Hammond, Cumberland, Orléans, Ottawa, Gatineau.
- [ ] **Primary category:** House cleaning service. **Secondary:** Commercial cleaning
      service, Window cleaning service.
- [ ] Phone **(343) 843-7761**, website **magicstickclean.ca**, booking link →
      booking.html. Hours matching reality.
- [ ] **Services** list with prices: Standard $30/h, Deep $43/h, Airbnb $30/h,
      Commercial $30/h, Windows (15% off), Post-construction $50/h, Move-in/out $50/h.
- [ ] **Photos:** logo, cover, 6–10 real before/after + work photos (your biggest
      trust booster vs. competitors with no photos).
- [ ] **Attributes** (only if TRUE): online appointments, on-site services,
      identifies as… (if you wish), languages: French & English.
- [ ] **Description (bilingual, keyword-rich):** e.g. "Magicstick Clean — ménage
      résidentiel et commercial à Rockland, Ottawa et Gatineau. Le même nettoyeur à
      chaque visite, prix clairs dès 30 $/h, réservation en ligne, service bilingue.
      Residential & commercial cleaning, same cleaner every visit, transparent pricing,
      online booking, bilingual."
- [ ] Seed 3–5 **Q&A** yourself (prices? areas? bilingual? book online? deposit?).

**The review flywheel (critical — you start at 0):** ask every satisfied client, make
it one tap, respond to every review. Get your short review link from the GBP dashboard
("Ask for reviews" → copy link) and put it in a saved text + a QR code on an invoice/
thank-you card. Target: **5 reviews in 30 days, 15 in 90 days.** That single number is
what beats Splendid and reassures people wary of a brand-new business.

## 6. Batch #4 — Review request scripts (never buy/fake reviews)

Ask right after a job the client is visibly happy with. Make it effortless; offer
nothing in exchange (against Google policy and it poisons trust).

**In person / text, right after the job**
- FR: « Merci beaucoup [Prénom] ! Si tu es content·e du résultat, un petit avis
  Google de 30 secondes nous aiderait énormément — on démarre à Rockland et chaque
  avis compte vraiment. Je t'envoie le lien, c'est un seul clic 🙏 »
- EN: "Thank you so much, [Name]! If you're happy with the result, a quick 30-second
  Google review would mean the world — we're just starting in Rockland and every
  review truly counts. I'll text you the one-tap link 🙏"

**Follow-up text with the link (send within the hour)**
- FR: « Bonjour [Prénom] ! Encore merci de nous avoir fait confiance 😊 Voici le lien
  pour laisser ton avis (1 clic) : [LIEN GOOGLE]. Ça prend 30 sec et ça aide un voisin
  à nous découvrir. Bonne journée ! — [Ton nom], Magicstick Clean »
- EN: "Hi [Name]! Thanks again for trusting us 😊 Here's the one-tap link to leave a
  review: [GOOGLE LINK]. Takes 30 sec and helps a neighbour find us. Have a great day!
  — [Your name], Magicstick Clean"

**If they hesitate / had a concern:** don't push for a public review — ask what could
be better, fix it, and win the next one. A problem solved privately becomes your best
future review.

## 7. Batch #5 — 30-day launch plan (0 → first clients → reviews)

Rhythm: post 2–3×/week, ask every happy client for a review, track leads daily.

**Week 1 — Foundations (no ad spend yet)**
- Set up & verify Google Business Profile (Batch #3). Create/brand FB + IG.
- Post #1, #2, #4 (kitchen, same-cleaner, local pride).
- Order a **vehicle decal** (rolling billboard). Print simple flyers/door hangers.
- Tell your whole network you're open; book your first 2–3 jobs (even discounted/
  friends) — you need real jobs to generate real reviews.

**Week 2 — Content + first paid test**
- Launch **Ad 1 (awareness)** at $15/day. Post #3, #5.
- Hand-deliver flyers in 2–3 Rockland neighbourhoods. Join & post in Rockland FB
  community groups (before/after).
- After each job → **review request** (Batch #6). Goal: first 3 reviews.

**Week 3 — Lead gen + partnerships**
- Switch/add **Ad 2 (free quote / price)**. Post #6, #7.
- Outreach: 5 realtors (move-in/out), 3 Airbnb hosts (turnover), 3 contractors
  (post-construction). Template: local, same-day, reliable, bilingual.
- Keep asking for reviews. Goal: 5–6 total reviews.

**Week 4 — Retarget + referral + double down**
- Launch **Ad 3 (retargeting/offer)** to site visitors & engagers. Post #8, #9.
- Launch a **referral program** (you set the honest reward). Post #10 (thank-you +
  review ask) now that you have happy clients.
- Review the numbers: keep the winning ad/creative, cut losers. Goal: 8–10 reviews,
  first recurring client signed.

**KPIs to watch weekly:** # quote requests, # bookings, cost per quote (from ads),
# Google reviews, # recurring plans. If cost per quote is low and reviews are growing,
pour more budget into the winner. That's the 0 → 100 engine.
