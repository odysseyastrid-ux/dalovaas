Official contest rules

“50 free cleanings to win” contest, Magicstick Clean.

  1. Organizer. Magicstick Clean, Clarence-Rockland (Ontario). Phone: (343) 843-7761 · Email: magicstickclean@gmail.com · Website: magicstickclean.ca
  2. Contest period. From October 13, 2026 to December 31, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. (Eastern Time).
  3. Eligibility. Residents of Clarence-Rockland (Ontario) aged 18 or older, for a home located in Clarence-Rockland. Employees of Magicstick Clean and members of their immediate family are not eligible.
  4. No purchase necessary. Entering is free.
  5. How to enter. Fill in the entry form on this page, or call (343) 843-7761 during the contest period. Limit: one entry per person and per address.
  6. Prizes. 50 prizes, each one standard 3-hour cleaning (approximate retail value $90), for a total value of $4,500. Prizes cannot be exchanged for cash, transferred or substituted. A prize must be used by June 30, 2027, subject to availability. Extras and add-ons are not included.
  7. Draw. On January 5, 2027 at 12 p.m. (Eastern Time), in Clarence-Rockland, at random among all eligible entries received. The odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received.
  8. Winners. Selected entrants will be contacted by phone or email within 7 days of the draw. Before being declared a winner, each selected entrant must correctly answer, without assistance, a mathematical skill-testing question. If a selected entrant cannot be reached or does not answer within 10 days, another entry will be drawn.
  9. Personal information. Used to run the contest and contact the winners. Magicstick Clean also uses entries internally to get to know its customers and improve its services (for example: which neighbourhoods and services are in demand, which ads work). Information is never sold or shared, in line with our privacy policy. Entering does not sign you up for anything: you receive our offers only if you tick the optional box on the form, and you can unsubscribe at any time by replying “unsubscribe” or calling (343) 843-7761. Entering is the same whether or not you tick it.
  10. Questions. (343) 843-7761 or magicstickclean@gmail.com.
