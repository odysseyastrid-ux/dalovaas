Clean more often, pay less per hour

Every visit starts at our standard rate of $30/h, with a 3-hour minimum. Choose a recurring plan and every visit drops to $27/h, that's 10% off, for as long as you keep it.

  1. One-time

    Standard rate

    $30/h

    A one-off visit usually means more built-up dirt and a home we're seeing for the first time, so each room takes longer. That's why it's billed at our full rate.

  2. Monthly

    10% off

    $27/h

    A fixed monthly slot lets us plan our schedule ahead and send the same cleaner every time. A month is long enough for dust to build up, and your recurring plan locks in the $27/h rate.

  3. Biweekly

    10% off

    $27/h

    Every two weeks, your home never falls far behind. Visits go faster because we're maintaining, not catching up, a good balance of budget and cleanliness for most busy households.

  4. Weekly

    10% off

    $27/h

    Your home stays clean all the time, so every visit is pure upkeep. Less work per visit and a guaranteed weekly spot on our calendar at the recurring $27/h rate.
