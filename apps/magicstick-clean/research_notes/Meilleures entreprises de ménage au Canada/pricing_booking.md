# Pricing models and booking experience of leading Canadian residential cleaning companies (2026)

Scope note: research used ~14 tool calls. Companies actually verified from their own pages: Hellamaid (Toronto), Molly Maid Canada, Merry Maids Canada (terms), Adèle Express (Montréal/Toronto/Ottawa), Ménage-Pro (Québec), The Montreal Cleaners / Les Nettoyeurs de Montréal. NOT verified (no data found): Maids Canada, Done Right Cleaning, Glebe Clean, BlueGuard, Clean & Shiny, Calgary and Vancouver independents. Some search hits were US companies and were discarded.

## Which pricing model dominates, and what are the published price ranges in 2026?

### Takeaway
Among the companies checked, the most transparent (Hellamaid) offers two published models side by side: a flat package built from a base + bedrooms/bathrooms/sq ft, and an hourly rate per cleaner with a 3-hour minimum. The franchise brands (Molly Maid, Merry Maids, Ménage-Pro) are quote-only; Adèle Express uses an online questionnaire that returns a custom price. Independent guides say experienced operators drift toward flat per-home prices, and typical Canadian visit totals sit around $160-$320.

### Cited Findings

Examples: 1 bed/1 bath $188, 2 bed/1 bath $208, 3 bed/2 bath $278, 4 bed/2 bath $318 (all before HST). Meta description: studios from $129, 1-bedroom from $188. Page modified 2026-11-16 — [Hellamaid Toronto](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)
- Hellamaid hourly option: $59 per cleaner per hour + HST, 3-hour minimum, 1 to 4 cleaners (1 cleaner x 3 h = $177; 2 cleaners x 3 h = $354). Hourly bookings use the client's own checklist; package bookings use the 50-point checklist — [Hellamaid Toronto](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)

### Inferences
- Pricing model by type of company: online-first companies (Hellamaid, Adèle) show or compute price on the site; franchises (Molly Maid, Merry Maids, Ménage-Pro) use quote-only. I could not verify a "dominant" model across all top-rated firms; the sample is small (6 companies).
- Hellamaid's per-cleaner hourly price ($59 + HST) is far above HomeStars' $35-$65 per-person range top and above solo rates, which suggests a premium positioning (living-wage employer, insured).
- Quebec independents/agencies price lower per hour ($25-$45) than Toronto/Vancouver figures.
- For a solo/owner-operated business in Ottawa-Gatineau, relevant benchmarks: Ottawa $32-$48/h solo (Biller), Montréal agencies $25-$45/h (Montreal Cleaners); both are consistent with a $39.50/h floor being mid-market.

### Gaps
- No published prices found for Maids Canada, Done Right Cleaning, Glebe Clean, BlueGuard, Clean & Shiny, or specific Vancouver/Calgary/Ottawa-Gatineau top independents; the search did not surface them.
- Molly Maid and Merry Maids Canadian price levels are not published; HomeStars ranges are the only proxy.
- No rating-ranked list of "best-rated" companies was verified; only self-reported ratings (Hellamaid 4.8 Google 400+ reviews, Adèle 4.7).

## Do the best show prices up front or quote only? What is the effect on conversion?

### Takeaway
Mixed: the online-first Canadian firms (Hellamaid, Adèle) show or compute a price before commitment ("no charge until after your clean"); the big franchises are quote-only with free estimates. Industry vendor blogs claim instant price and fast response raise conversion, but the figures are vendor-sourced and general, not Canada- or cleaning-specific.

### Cited Findings
- Hellamaid markets "Upfront online pricing" and "No charge until after your clean"; the price widget asks type, size, bedrooms, bathrooms and then name/email/phone before showing the price ("See my price") — [Hellamaid Toronto](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)
- The Montreal Cleaners explicitly argues for publishing ranges ("most companies refuse to show prices") but still requires a written quote for the exact price — [The Montreal Cleaners](https://www.themontrealcleaners.ca/fr/honoraires-femme-menage-montreal-laval-longueuil/)
- Estimatty (cleaning-estimate software vendor, Aug 2026): leads responded to within 5 minutes are ~21x more likely to qualify than after 30 minutes (MIT/InsideSales study); form conversion falls from 18.2% (1 field) to 13.0% (2), 11.5% (3), 8.1% (5), 4.2% (9+) (Neil Patel summary); advice to show a price range early and ask phone number clearly — [Estimatty](https://www.estimatty.com/blog/how-to-improve-conversion-rates)
- Estimatty also cites a Sales Cadia benchmark (response within 1 minute raises conversion 391% vs 5 minutes); this is a secondary vendor claim, unverified here — [Estimatty](https://www.estimatty.com/blog/how-to-improve-conversion-rates)
- QuoteIQ (software vendor) positions self-quoting so "a homeowner can get a real price at 10 PM without calling"; no conversion statistic captured — [QuoteIQ](https://myquoteiq.com/best-customer-self-quoting-software-cleaning-businesses-2026/)
- Hellamaid collects contact details before showing the price (lead capture gate), so "upfront" is not anonymous — [Hellamaid Toronto](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)

### Inferences
- Speed-of-reply and short forms are the best-supported levers; the evidence for "showing a price" specifically is anecdotal/vendor-driven.
- A same-day reply promise is consistent with this evidence but a 5-minute reply is the cited benchmark.

### Gaps
- No independent (non-vendor) study quantifying conversion of price-visible vs quote-only cleaning sites in Canada found.
- Original sources of the 21x and 391% statistics were not opened.

## Booking features (instant booking, same-day reply, online payment, reminders, recurring, gift cards)

### Takeaway
Hellamaid and Adèle offer true online booking and payment flows; Molly Maid, Merry Maids and Ménage-Pro are lead-form + callback models. Recurring scheduling is universal; gift cards appear at some firms.

### Cited Findings
- Hellamaid: online booking with price; pay after the clean; recurring weekly/bi-weekly/monthly; phone support 7 am-7 pm daily; condo access notes (buzzer, lockbox, concierge) collected in the booking; 4,000+ Toronto homes, 4.8 Google (400+ reviews), $5M liability insurance, background checks — [Hellamaid Toronto](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)
- Adèle Express: 3 steps: get price from questionnaire, book and pay online (Visa, Mastercard, Interac-type logos shown), then the "Pro" arrives; "on-demand or subscription" — [Adèle Express](https://adeleexpress.com/)
- Molly Maid Canada: inquiry form only, no online price or payment visible — [Molly Maid Canada](https://mollymaid.ca/); US-site quote form also asks for opt-in to SMS service reminders — [Molly Maid US form](https://www.mollymaid.com/request-a-free-estimate/)
- Merry Maids: site does not collect payment at booking; a quote is sent, local franchise calls to confirm price and schedule; recurring clients' card is kept on file and charged in advance of each cleaning; arrival windows rather than exact times (US-template terms) — [Merry Maids terms](https://merrymaids.ca/terms-of-use)
- The Montreal Cleaners: gift certificates offered ("Certificats-cadeaux de nettoyage" page in navigation); accepts Visa, Mastercard, Interac, cheque, direct deposit; trial clean possible before long-term engagement — [The Montreal Cleaners](https://www.themontrealcleaners.ca/fr/honoraires-femme-menage-montreal-laval-longueuil/)
- Ménage-Pro: free estimate visit, online estimate request, phone — no online payment seen — [Ménage-Pro](https://www.menagepro.ca/)

### Inferences
- Customer portal / reminder features could not be confirmed from public pages for any Canadian firm; do not claim them as industry standard.

### Gaps
- Response-time promises (same-day, within X hours) were only found for The Montreal Cleaners (quote within 24 h / 48 h). Not found for others.
- Customer portal and automated reminders: not verified.

## Common policies: cancellation, minimums, recurring discounts, guarantees, lockout fees

### Takeaway
Typical Canadian policy: 24-hour free cancellation (Hellamaid), up to 72 hours at franchise level (Merry Maids), 3-hour minimum for hourly, recurring discounts 10-20%, and a re-clean guarantee within 24 hours. Lockout fees were not found for Canadian companies (only US examples).

### Cited Findings
- Hellamaid: free cancel/reschedule with 24+ hours' notice; "Missed something? We come back and fix it"; 100% satisfaction; 3-hour minimum on hourly; weekly 20% / bi-weekly 15% / monthly 10% off from the 2nd recurring booking — [Hellamaid Toronto](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)
- Merry Maids (terms of a US-template site): 72 hours' notice to avoid a fee, up to full price if shorter notice; recurring-service cancellation fee capped at $200; "Worry Free Guarantee": notify within 24 h and team re-cleans at no charge; skipping multiple visits may adjust price (frequency-based pricing); breakage reported within 24 h; cleaners won't move items over 20 lb or climb above a two-step ladder — [Merry Maids](https://merrymaids.ca/terms-of-use)
- The Montreal Cleaners: no minimum number of visits, no excessive cancellation fees, no annual automatic increases, floor price $85 per visit; new-client discounts "regularly" — [The Montreal Cleaners](https://www.themontrealcleaners.ca/fr/honoraires-femme-menage-montreal-laval-longueuil/)
- Adèle Express: no contract, no commitment — [Adèle Express](https://adeleexpress.com/)
- Molly Maid Canada: 100% satisfaction guarantee ("We'll make it right") — [Molly Maid Canada](https://mollymaid.ca/)
- HomeStars: companies often require contracts; frequency saves $7-$15 per visit; contact the company within 24 h if not satisfied and most re-clean free; tipping $10-$20 (10-15%) not expected — [HomeStars](https://www.homestars.com/cleaning/price-guides/house-apartment-cleaning-cost)
- Hellamaid blog snippet: median bi-weekly appointment $162.26 vs weekly $149.16 in Ontario (source says rates per cleaner-hour: weekly $50.88, bi-weekly $52.83, every four weeks $57.63) — [Hellamaid blog](https://hellamaid.ca/blog/weekly-vs-biweekly-cleaning-cost/) (snippet only; not opened)
- Biller.ca: recurring 10-20% off; monthly little or none — [Biller.ca](https://biller.ca/pricing-guides/how-much-to-charge-house-cleaning-canada)
- US examples only (not Canada, for contrast): $50 late-cancel fee under 24 h; $80 late-cancel and $119 lockout at another operator — [Good Maid](https://goodmaidcleaning.com/terms), [Maid Marines](https://maidmarines.com/terms)

### Inferences
- A 24-hour free cancellation window, 3-hour minimum, and first-visit-at-regular-price/recurring-discount-thereafter are the clearest Canadian-market conventions seen.
- Charging the first deep clean at 1.5-2x (Biller) vs a flat +$99 add-on (Hellamaid) are two different ways to price the first visit.

### Gaps
- No Canadian company with a published lockout fee found.
- Satisfaction guarantee terms for Hellamaid (time window) not stated on the page reviewed.
- Taxes: HST 13% (Ontario) and GST 5% + QST 9.975% (Québec) are shown as extra on the pages above; Alberta/BC not checked.
