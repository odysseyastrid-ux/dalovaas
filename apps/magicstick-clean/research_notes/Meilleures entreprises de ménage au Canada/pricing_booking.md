## Which pricing model dominates and what are published prices?

### Takeaway
Hellamaid (Toronto) publishes a hybrid: flat package by home size (base + per bedroom/bathroom/sq ft) OR hourly per cleaner, with prices shown online. Franchise brands (Molly Maid, Merry Maids) use free-estimate / quote forms. Independent guide data (HomeStars, Biller.ca) puts typical Canadian visit at about $160-$320.

### Cited Findings
- Hellamaid Toronto: package base $99 before HST; examples 1bed/1bath $188, 2bed/1bath $208, 3bed/2bath $278, 4bed/2bath $318 (before HST); studio from $129, 1-bed from $188 — [Hellamaid Toronto](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)
- Hellamaid hourly: $59 per cleaner per hour + HST, 3-hour minimum, 1 to 4 cleaners (3h x 1 cleaner = $177) — [Hellamaid Toronto](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)
- Hellamaid add-ons: Deep clean +$99, Move-in/out +$159, inside oven +$29, inside fridge +$29, inside cabinets +$39, interior windows+tracks from +$39, pets +$29, Airbnb +$95; 13% HST in Ontario — [Hellamaid Toronto](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)
- Hellamaid recurring discounts: weekly 20%, bi-weekly 15%, monthly 10%, starting from the second recurring booking (first clean at regular rate) — [Hellamaid Toronto](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)
- HomeStars 2026: average Canadian home cleaning about $230 (range $160-$320); hourly $65-$125 (visit, often multiple cleaners); deep clean one-time $270-$410; move-in/out $260-$450; $35-$65 per person/hour for companies; frequency saves about $7-$15 per cleaning — [HomeStars](https://www.homestars.com/cleaning/price-guides/house-apartment-cleaning-cost)
- Biller.ca (Nov 2026): solo cleaners $28-$55/h; company team visits $65-$125/h; Toronto $35-$55/h, Vancouver $40-$55/h, Calgary/Edmonton $30-$45, Ottawa $32-$48 (compiled from public guides, July 2026) — [Biller.ca](https://biller.ca/pricing-guides/how-much-to-charge-house-cleaning-canada)
- Biller.ca: experienced cleaners move to flat per-home rates; deep clean 1.5-2x standard; recurring weekly/bi-weekly 10-20% off; monthly little discount — [Biller.ca](https://biller.ca/pricing-guides/how-much-to-charge-house-cleaning-canada)
