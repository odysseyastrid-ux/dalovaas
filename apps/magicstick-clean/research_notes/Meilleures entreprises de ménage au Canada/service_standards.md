# Service standards of top-rated residential cleaning companies in Canada (2026) — PARTIAL, in progress

Research date: 2026-11-24. Many company sites block automated fetches (403/404); figures below are only what could be verified. Google review counts for most companies could NOT be verified (Google Maps not fetchable) — marked unknown.

## Which companies have the highest Google/other ratings and review counts?

### Takeaway
Verified numbers are sparse. Hellamaid self-reports 5.0 on Google (492 reviews) on its own site; independent Trustpilot shows 4.4 (53 reviews). Hire A Maid (Toronto) shows 9.8/10 from 337 reviews on HomeStars; Maids 4U Canada shows 4.8 from 68 Google reviews. Big franchises (Molly Maid, Merry Maids) publish no rating figures on pages fetched.

### Cited Findings
- Hellamaid homepage displays "Google rating of 5.0 based on 492 reviews" (self-reported, date seen 2026-11-24; no independent confirmation) — [hellamaid.com](https://hellamaid.com)
- Hellamaid on Trustpilot: 4.4/5, 53 reviews (40 in last 12 months), 87% 5-star and 13% 1-star; newest review Nov 18 2026; company replies to 100% of negative reviews — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hellamaid.com)
- Hire A Maid (Toronto) HomeStars average 9.8/10 from 337 reviews (as listed in search snippet, date unknown) — [HomeStars](https://sp-homer.homestars.com/companies/2808756-hire-a-maid)
- Maids 4U Canada Google Business listing: 4.8 from 68 reviews (snippet, date unknown) — [Google listing](https://business.google.com/v/maids-4u-canada-ltd/05154984683626626778/4562/_/rev)
- Molly Maid Canada main page shows testimonials but no star rating/review totals — [mollymaid.ca](https://www.mollymaid.ca)
- "Best of" lists (ThreeBestRated, which says it uses its own 50-point inspection) are promotional/curated, not independent; Montreal names listed: Montreal Maid Services, Nettoyage Pro Cleaning, Le Chiffon Vert, Douzinet — [ThreeBestRated Montréal](https://threebestrated.ca/fr-services-de-nettoyage-de-maison-in-montreal-qc)

### Inferences
- Self-reported Google 5.0 vs Trustpilot 4.4 for the same company suggests Google figures on company sites should be checked live.

### Recurring practices (inference from above)
- Common: satisfaction/re-clean promise (24 h to 2 business days to report), "insured/bonded", background check claims, published checklist or numbered process, online booking with SMS/email reminders, rating requests after clean, eco products as option.
- Rarely published: exact re-clean terms, WSIB/WCB/CNESST, same-cleaner assignment, photo reports, bilingual service, arrival windows.
