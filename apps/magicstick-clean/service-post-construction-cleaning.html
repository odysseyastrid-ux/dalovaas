Post-Construction & Event Cleanup

A one-time deep reset after a renovation, new build, or event — clearing fine dust and debris so the space is ready to use or show.

What's included

  • Fine construction dust removed from surfaces, sills and vents
  • Floors swept, vacuumed and mopped, including debris from the work area
  • Windows, mirrors and fixtures wiped clean of dust and residue
  • Kitchens and bathrooms cleaned if part of the renovated area
  • Trash and leftover packaging removed

Who it's for

Homeowners after a renovation, contractors handing a space back to a client, or anyone who's hosted an event and needs the space reset quickly. This is typically a one-time visit, though we're happy to become your recurring cleaner afterward if the space needs regular upkeep — see Standard Cleaning.

How it works

01

Dust removed first

Fine construction dust settles everywhere — it's cleared from surfaces, sills and vents before anything else.

02

Deep floor clean

Floors are swept, vacuumed and mopped to clear leftover debris and residue from the work area.

03

A finished, move-in look

Windows, fixtures and any renovated kitchens or bathrooms get a final polish so the space looks done, not just tidied.

A clear quote before you commit

Tell us the size of the space and the extent of the work done, and we'll give you a straightforward price with no surprises.
