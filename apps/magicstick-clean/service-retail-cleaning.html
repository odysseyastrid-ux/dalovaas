Retail & Store Cleaning

Floors, entrances and fitting rooms kept customer-ready — scheduled before opening, after closing, or whenever suits your store.

What's included

  • Aisle and entrance floors swept, mopped or vacuumed
  • Fitting rooms and checkout counters wiped down
  • Glass doors and display windows cleaned
  • Washrooms cleaned and restocked, if on-site
  • Trash removal and bin re-lining

Who it's for

Storefronts and small retail spaces in Clarence-Rockland, Ottawa and Gatineau that need a presentable, customer-ready floor every day without interrupting business hours. Visits are scheduled around your opening and closing times, on whatever recurring basis fits your store.

How it works

01

Around your hours

Visits are scheduled before opening, after closing, or during a quiet stretch — whatever keeps the store customer-ready.

02

03

A consistent standard

The same cleaner keeps your store looking the same way, visit after visit.
