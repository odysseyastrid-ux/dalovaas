Standard House Cleaning

Regular upkeep that keeps your home consistently clean between deeper visits — the same cleaner, every time, across Clarence-Rockland, Ottawa & Gatineau.

What's included

  • Kitchen: counters, stovetop, sink, exterior of appliances, and washing dishes left out
  • Bathrooms: toilets, sinks, showers/tubs, mirrors, and floors
  • Dusting of reachable surfaces, shelves and furniture
  • Vacuuming carpets and mopping hard floors throughout
  • Emptying trash and re-lining bins
A real hardwood floor reset from a standard visit

Who it's for

Standard Cleaning is the right fit if your home just needs regular upkeep — most clients book it weekly, biweekly, or monthly. Recurring visits come with a standing discount (up to 15% off for weekly service), and you'll always see a clear price before booking. If it's been a while since the last proper clean, or you're preparing for guests, our Deep Cleaning service is a better starting point.

How your visit goes

01

Kitchen & bathrooms first

The rooms that get dirtiest fastest are cleaned top to bottom, including anything left in the sink.

02

Dust, then vacuum, then mop

Surfaces and shelves are dusted before floors are vacuumed and mopped, so nothing settles back down.

03

A quick walkthrough with you

Anything missed gets fixed on the spot — or within 24 hours if you notice it after I've left.

Eco-friendly, brought by me

Supplies and equipment are included at no extra cost, and they're eco-friendly by default. If you'd rather I use products you already have at home, just mention it when you book.
